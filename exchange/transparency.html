Fees

  • 0.30% per swap, paid by the trader — the standard Uniswap V2 fee.
  • Of that, 0.25% goes to liquidity providers and 0.05% to the platform fee address (when the platform fee is on).
  • No deposit, withdrawal or listing fees. Network gas is paid by the user to Base, not to us.

What the admin can and cannot do

The admin (fee-setter) address can only:

  • turn the 0.05% platform fee on or off and change its recipient;
  • hand this role to another address.

The admin cannot:

  • move, freeze or seize anyone's tokens or liquidity;
  • pause trading, block users or change swap prices;
  • upgrade or change the contract code — the contracts are immutable.

The Factory and Pair contracts have no other privileged functions. Their code is the public Uniswap V2 source; source verification on the block explorer will be added after deployment so anyone can check this directly.

Risks

  • Anyone can list a token. Fake or malicious tokens may appear. Always verify a token's contract address from its official source.
  • Price impact & MEV. Small pools move a lot on each trade; bots may front-run. Keep slippage tolerance low.
  • Impermanent loss. Liquidity providers can end up with less value than simply holding.
  • Smart-contract risk. Even audited code can have undiscovered bugs, and immutable contracts cannot be patched.
  • Website risk. Always check you are on agichainai.com. We will never ask for your seed phrase.
